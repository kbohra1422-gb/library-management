📖 Managing Books

1. How do I add a new book to the library?

Use the Add New Book form on the left side of the Dashboard. Fill in the Title, Author, Category, and optional initial Status, then click Add Book to Library.

2. How do I search or filter books?

Use the filter bar above the Book List on the Dashboard. You can search keywords in book titles, authors, or categories, or filter specifically by Category and Status.

3. How do I issue or return a book?

In the Book List table, click the Issue button to mark an available book as borrowed, or click Return to mark a borrowed book as available.

4. How do I edit or delete a book?

Click the Edit button in the action column to update title, author, category, or status. Click Delete to permanently remove a book entry.

5. Where is the data stored?

All book records are persistently saved in the local library.db SQLite database file.
